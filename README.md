2026 in three panels: the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya with no
deal, overnight Russian strikes killing at least 12 people in Ukraine's Kyiv
region, and the Java candy-factory fire with 24 confirmed dead as the search
resumed on Monday. Sources are listed in `build/SOURCES.md` in the workspace;
the image itself is `entry.png`, and `entry/main.ts` copies it to the output
directory.
